The bottom of the radio shows your callsign, an Options button, and the version. Options is where you change keys, turn the overlay and the channel wheel on or off, paste or forget the admin key for this community, choose sounds, turn on simple mode, set up a phone as the talk button, and check for updates.

Default keys are still F1 to talk (hold it), F2 for the channel wheel, F3 for the previous channel, F4 for the next channel, and F5 to hide or show the overlay. Keys you already changed stay as they are. Keys you never changed switch to these the next time you open the app.

The Windows helper is a separate download, not this installer. The app asks before it downloads an update. The keyboard hook stays off until you accept the privacy note. The installer is unsigned: on the SmartScreen warning choose More info, then Run anyway. SHA256SUMS.txt next to the installer is the checksum of this file.
