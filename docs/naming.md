*Prices: Cloudflare Registrar (sells at cost, renewal = registration, free WHOIS privacy, needs Cloudflare DNS, which suits us anyway) and Porkbun, in USD, converted at 1 USD ≈ NZ$1.79 (9 Oct). .com goes up ~7% on 1 Nov 2026.*

## Name ideas

| Name | Why | Watch-outs |
|---|---|---|
| **Squelch** | The radio control that cuts the hiss so you only hear real transmissions. "Clear comms" in one word. | `squelch.com/.dev/.app` are taken, so we'd use `getsquelch.com` |
| **Netcall** | Literally what you do: call on a net. Plain, says what it is. | `netcall.com` taken; possible confusion with generic "net call" |
| **Freqline** | "Freq" is how players talk ("what freq is arty on?") + a clean line. | `freqline.com` taken; `.app`/`.dev` free |
| **TacNet** | Short, milsim feel, easy to say in voice. | Generic term, likely used by other products, so trademark risk is higher |
| **Sideband** | Radio term, sounds good. | ⚠️ An existing open-source messaging app is already called Sideband (Reticulum). I'd avoid it. |
| *(keep)* **Radio Net** | Works, self-explanatory | Generic; `radionet.com/.app` taken |

My pick: **Squelch**, with **Freqline** as runner-up.
