# Voice provider comparison for Radio Net

*Checked 9 Oct 2026 (NZ time). Prices are the vendors' public list prices at that date, in the vendor's currency. "PCU" = peak concurrent users in a month.*

**Our workload:** 10–20 people, ~3 nets each, ~3 h/night, ~3 nights/week. About 20 peak users and **~120,000 participant-minutes/month** if every net connection is billed separately (20 users × ~2.5 nets × 180 min × 13 nights).

**Critical requirement:** one person can **listen to several nets at once and transmit on exactly one**, on a **Windows desktop** app outside the game.

## What WARDOGS uses (evidence)

- **Vivox.** The in-game error *"Failed to connect to vivox communication server. Voice Chat unavailable."* appears in the official Steam Report-a-Problem forum, with Team17 support replying: [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/discussions/3/571549538645524618/).
- BULKHEAD's CEO, on the Push to Talk podcast, on tweeting about "Vivox going down" and having to use it for "privacy reasons": [transcript](https://exa.ai/library/podcast/jsmbhb1ydgx/episode/07mcfpp2y2q).
- Outage threads: [Reddit, Aug 2026](https://www.reddit.com/r/WarDogs/comments/1vivltj/the_devs_really_need_to_reconsider_their_prox/), [Steam "VOIP is down", 29 Sep](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1867240/discussions/0/585061437343354864/).
- Unofficial: one fan/troubleshooting site says the build ships the Vivox plugin plus **atmoky TrueSpatial** for positional audio ([beakmode.app](https://beakmode.app/wardogs-voice-chat-not-working)). This is not confirmed by an official source.
- I did not find official Steam-page credits or third-party notices listing Vivox. The confidence that it's Vivox is still **high**, based on the in-game error string plus the CEO's comments.

## Cost notes / uncertainty

- Minute-billed providers (Agora, Daily, 100ms, Twilio, LiveKit Cloud) all land at **~US$50–480/mo** for us. Self-hosting or per-peak-user billing (ODIN, Vivox) is far cheaper for a small, heavy-use group.
- If each person joins only 1–2 nets, the minute estimate drops by a third or more. That still doesn't change the ranking.
- Twilio's current per-minute price and Agora/Daily multi-connection specifics weren't re-verified in depth, because they were never contenders on cost.
