Radio Net: UI direction (static mockup)

Calm dark graphite with two signal colours: green = live / your TX channel, red = you are on air. Frequencies are always in a monospace face so they read like a radio dial. Every action is one click or one key: tune by typing 59.5 or command, click "Transmit here", or press G for the channel wheel (radial-wheel.png). No radio effects, no fake knobs.

Main window. Left: your communities (like Discord servers). Middle: the community's channel list, with tune box and admin "+ New". Right: your radio, a card per tuned channel with volume, ear (L/C/R) and "Transmit here". The big bar always says where you'll talk; it turns red while you're keyed.

In-game overlay: a separate see-through, click-through window in a screen corner (nothing injected). On by default. Empty and invisible when nobody is transmitting. While someone transmits, one line: their display name and the channel (frequency + name). Several talkers stack. F10 hides it. Position, size and opacity in settings.
